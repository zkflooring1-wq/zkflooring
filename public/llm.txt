## Core Services

- [Carpet & Carpet Tile Fitting](https://zkflooring.co.uk/services/carpet-fitting): Luxury domestic twist, Saxony, Berber, wool carpets, stair runners with whipped edges, acoustic high-TOG PU foam underlays, and heavy contract commercial carpet tiles.
- [Luxury Vinyl Tile (LVT) & Sheet Vinyl](https://zkflooring.co.uk/services/luxury-vinyl-tile): 100% waterproof gluedown and click-lock LVT, specializing in intricate herringbone patterns, feature perimeter borders, and heavy-duty sheet vinyl.
- [Self Levelling & Subfloor Preparation](https://zkflooring.co.uk/services/subfloor-preparation): British Standard BS 8203 compliant subfloor preparation, including moisture testing, liquid DPM damp-proof membranes, SP101 ply boarding, and laser-flat latex self-levelling screeds.
- [Solid & Engineered Hardwood Flooring](https://zkflooring.co.uk/services/hardwood-flooring): Premium European oak engineered boards, solid timber planks, parquet herringbone blocks with acoustic underlays and expansion profiling.
- [Laminate Flooring Installation](https://zkflooring.co.uk/services/laminate-flooring): AC4 and AC5 commercial-grade scratch-resistant laminate flooring with authentic embossed woodgrain finishes.
- [Commercial Safety Flooring](https://zkflooring.co.uk/services/commercial-flooring): Slip-resistant vinyl (Altro, Polyflor), hot-welded seams, cap-and-cove skirting for commercial kitchens, clinics, schools, and hospitals.
- **Additional Carpentry & Interior Services**: Wall acoustic panelling, outdoor composite decking, and custom fitted cupboards and storage.

## Key Facts & Answers (AEO)

- **Free Home Survey**: ZK Flooring offers 100% free home surveys across Birmingham and the Midlands. The team brings a mobile showroom with hundreds of carpet, LVT, and laminate samples directly to customer premises, accompanied by precision laser measuring.
- **Standards Compliance**: All installations comply with British Standards: BS 8203 (Code of practice for installation of resilient floor coverings) and BS 5325 (Code of practice for installation of textile floor coverings).
- **Underfloor Heating (UFH)**: Full compatibility assessment. Gluedown LVT adheres to maximum 27°C surface temperature limits, and carpet/underlay combinations are engineered to stay strictly below 2.5 TOG for optimal heat transfer.
- **Quotes & Turnaround**: Fast quotations within 2 hours of inquiry. Typical domestic installations completed within 1 to 2 days including subfloor prep.
